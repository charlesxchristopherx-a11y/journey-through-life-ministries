Meet Our Founder

23+ Years Walking Alongside Women in Every Season

Rev. Georgetta is an ordained minister and Christian Empowerment & Budgeting Coach with more than 23 years of women's ministry experience. She works with empty-nesters, working moms, single parents, entrepreneurs, divorcees, widows, and women at every stage of life — using a seven-step biblical framework to help them discover their self-worth and identity in Christ.
