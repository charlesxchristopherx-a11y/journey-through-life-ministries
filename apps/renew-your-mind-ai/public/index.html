03 / Thoughtful boundaries

What “safe actions” means

01

Small and doable

A brief prayer, one journal prompt, a calming pause, one practical step, or reaching out to someone trustworthy.

02

No major decisions

The AI will not tell someone to change treatment, leave a relationship or job, give money, punish themselves, or take a dangerous action.

03

Human help comes first

Language suggesting self-harm or immediate danger is directed to real-time crisis and emergency support instead of an ordinary exercise.
