About

Meet Rev. Georgetta

Wife, mother, grandmother — and for more than 23 years, a guide for women rediscovering who God says they are.

Founder & Coach

Rev. Georgetta

Christian Empowerment & Budgeting Coach, ordained minister, and conference speaker offering online group coaching. For more than 23 years, Rev. Georgetta has walked alongside women in every season of life — empty-nesters, working moms, single parents, entrepreneurs, divorcees, widows, married women, and retirees — using a seven-step biblical framework to help them discover their self-worth and identity in Christ.

She was called to women's ministry in 2003 and expanded into virtual ministry in 2015, reaching women wherever they are. Outside of ministry, she's a wife, mother, and grandmother who enjoys knitting, crocheting, and paper crafts.

Our Mission

Free in Christ Jesus

We love teaching and ministering the freedom found only in Jesus Christ — helping women to know God and to grow in His Word, living the life He intended for them to live. Free in Christ Jesus!
