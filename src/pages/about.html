About Us

Living Life Confidently, Authentically & Victoriously

Our desire is to teach the love of God and the freedom found only in Jesus Christ — helping men and women know God and grow in His Word, living the life He intended: free in Christ Jesus.

Founder

Rev. Georgetta

Christian Empowerment & Budgeting Coach and conference speaker offering online group coaching. For more than 23 years, Rev. Georgetta has walked alongside women in every season of life — empty-nesters, working moms, single parents, entrepreneurs, divorcees, widows, married, and retired women — using a seven-step biblical framework to help them discover their self-worth and identity in Christ.

She created the ALIVE Reset & Restart Dream It Journal, a 90-day life-planning tool that pairs practical goal-setting with spiritual growth.

Men's Ministry

Elder Charles

Elder Charles heads our Men's Ministry. He ministers at local homeless shelters, produces devotional videos, and writes a devotional article series called "Life Messages." He speaks regularly at local churches and is available for wedding and funeral officiating, as well as chaplaincy services at nursing homes.
