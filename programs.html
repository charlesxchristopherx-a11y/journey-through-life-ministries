The ALIVE Program

ALIVE Living With Purpose Community

A quarterly coaching workshop — 27 workbooks across 9 modules — guiding you through healing, identity, and purpose in Christ.

How It Works

Pre-Recorded Teaching + Live Community

Each workbook pairs pre-recorded video teaching with one live Zoom session, so you get both flexible, go-at-your-own-pace learning and real-time connection with your coach and community.

01

Healing for a Wounded Soul

Understanding how God helps us process pain and begin the healing journey.

02

Letting Go, Letting God

Finding freedom from past wounds and learning to maintain your healing.

03

God's Plan, Our Purpose

Building spiritual strength with Christ as your foundation.

04

Freedom Is Ours & Dressed for Success

Understanding the distinction between bondage and freedom, and dressing for victorious living.

05–09

Progressive Biblical Studies

Life lessons from the kings, scripture interpretation, the discipline of prayer, and studies through the Books of Matthew and Psalms.
